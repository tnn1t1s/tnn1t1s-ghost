# Footage palette

Real-world clips for the GHOST "context band" video format (the `/ghost-context-band`
skill stacks a horizontal slice of one of these over a VCV rack performance). The
band gives a track a *world*; the footage choice does narrative work the audio
can't. Pick by mood, not just availability.

Rights note: these are reference sources for *personal/demo* edits during the soft
launch. Clear licensing before any monetized or widely-distributed use — most
archival channels license their footage.

## What makes a good clip
- **Eye-level human/street content in the middle third** — that's the band you
  usually crop, so the action should live there, not the sky or pavement.
- **Loose, ambient motion** (walking, traffic, water, machinery) over fast cuts —
  it should sit *under* the rhythm, not compete with it.
- **A point of view**: decay, transit, crowds, labor, night. The juxtaposition is
  the story. A 909 track over the right world says something a void can't.
- **Full-width or easily de-pillarboxed** (the skill strips side bars, but native
  16:9 content is cleanest).
